Freelancing in the United States

The United States has the largest freelance workforce in absolute terms and the highest rates. Payments are simple; tax is where new freelancers get caught, because nobody withholds anything and self-employment tax comes on top of income tax. This page covers the structure, with a rate calculator in dollars.

Getting paid in United States

Domestic clients pay by ACH bank transfer, check or Zelle, and Upwork and Fiverr pay straight into a US bank account. International clients pay through PayPal, Wise or Payoneer. If you work with clients abroad, the payment fee comparison shows what each route leaves you.

Where US freelancers find work

Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com, Toptal at the high end, and increasingly direct work through LinkedIn and Contra.

Registration and tax, at a structural level

  • Tax ID. No general freelancer licence at the federal level, though some cities and states require a local business licence. Most freelancers use their Social Security Number, or an EIN if they prefer not to give clients their SSN.
  • Income tax. Freelance income is reported on Schedule C with Form 1040. Clients paying $600 or more send a 1099-NEC, but all income is reportable either way. Self-employment tax of 15.3 percent of net earnings is added on Schedule SE, covering Social Security and Medicare.
  • Sales tax. There is no federal VAT. States levy sales tax on some goods and, in a minority of states, on certain services; freelance services sold to out-of-state or foreign clients are generally outside it.
  • Filing. Annual Form 1040 with Schedules C and SE. Quarterly estimated payments (Form 1040-ES) if you expect to owe $1,000 or more, due mid-April, mid-June, mid-September and mid-January.

Official source: Internal Revenue Service. The tax set-aside calculator's US mode includes self-employment tax.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my tax bill so much higher than when I was employed?

Because self-employment tax adds 15.3 percent on top of income tax, covering the Social Security and Medicare contributions an employer used to split with you. The tax set-aside calculator's US mode includes it.

Do I owe quarterly taxes?

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year, yes. Set aside a share of every payment and send estimated payments four times a year to avoid penalties.

I did not get a 1099. Do I still report the income?

Yes. The 1099 is the client's paperwork, not the trigger for your obligation. All freelance income is reportable.

Should I get an EIN?

It is free and keeps your Social Security Number off client paperwork. Many freelancers get one for that reason alone.
