Freelancing in Indonesia

Indonesia's freelance market splits in two. Freelancers on Sribu, Projects.co.id and Fastwork work with local clients in rupiah and search in Bahasa Indonesia; freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr work in English and are paid in dollars. This English page is for the second group, and an Indonesian-language version of the core tools is on the way. The rate calculator below is set to rupiah.

Getting paid in Indonesia

Local clients pay by bank transfer, which is the default on almost every Indonesian invoice. E-wallets such as GoPay, DANA and OVO dominate everyday payments but are used for small informal jobs rather than formal invoices. Foreign clients pay through Wise (usually the cheapest), PayPal (convenient, higher fees) or Payoneer (built into Fiverr), all settling into a rupiah bank account; moving that money into an e-wallet means withdrawing to the bank first. Where Indonesian freelancers find work

Sribu (which absorbed Sribulancer), Projects.co.id and Fastwork for local clients. Upwork and Fiverr for foreign clients, where English fluency is the gating skill.

Registration and tax, at a structural level

  • Tax ID. An NPWP (taxpayer number) is needed to file and to use the simplified regime. Since 2022 most citizens' 16-digit NIK doubles as their NPWP.
  • Income tax. Freelancers with annual turnover under Rp4.8 billion can use the simplified UMKM regime: a final tax of 0.5 percent of gross turnover, paid monthly, with the first Rp500 million exempt. Certain independent professions (lawyers, accountants, consultants, architects and others) are excluded and use the progressive rates instead. Corporate clients may withhold PPh 21 or PPh 23 at source.
  • VAT (PPN). Registration as a PKP becomes mandatory once turnover passes Rp4.8 billion a year, after which 11 percent PPN is charged on invoices. Almost no solo freelancer crosses this.
  • Filing. Annual return (SPT Tahunan) by 31 March through the DJP online system. The 0.5 percent final tax is paid as a monthly instalment. Documents stating a value over Rp5,000,000, including invoices and contracts, need a Rp10,000 e-meterai stamp.

Official source: Direktorat Jenderal Pajak. The tax set-aside calculator has a simple mode for any country; its US mode does not apply to Indonesia.

Frequently asked questions

Do I put my NPWP on invoices?

Yes. Corporate clients need it to process their own withholding and expense records, and an invoice without it is often sent back.

What is the 0.5 percent final tax?

A simplified regime for turnover under Rp4.8 billion: 0.5 percent of gross monthly turnover, no expense bookkeeping, first Rp500 million exempt. Independent professions listed by the tax office are excluded and pay progressive rates.

What is e-meterai and when do I need it?

An electronic stamp duty of Rp10,000 required on civil documents, including invoices and contracts, that state a value above Rp5,000,000. Skipping it carries a 100 percent penalty.

Which foreign payment method is cheapest?

Wise is usually the lowest total cost because it converts at the mid-market rate. PayPal costs more but connects to more services. Put your typical invoice into the fee comparison tool.
