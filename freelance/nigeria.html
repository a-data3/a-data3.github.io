Freelancing in Nigeria

Nigeria leads the fastest-growing freelance region in the world. The hard parts are getting foreign currency in without losing it to conversion, and keeping up with tax rules that changed in 2025. This page lays out the structure, with a rate calculator in naira.

Getting paid in Nigeria

Flutterwave is the most-used local service, accepting card and bank payments in many currencies and paying out by transfer, card or USSD. Payoneer is common too, either converting to naira automatically or paying into a domiciliary (foreign-currency) account at banks such as GTBank or Zenith so you can hold dollars and convert when the rate suits you. Newer dollar-wallet apps compete on the naira conversion rate. The payment fee comparison lets you put each route's real fee and markup side by side.

Where Nigerian freelancers find work

Upwork and Fiverr dominate. Nigerian platforms include Proville, Asuqu, Jolancer, PBridge (naira escrow) and Terawork.

Registration and tax, at a structural level

  • Tax ID. A Tax Identification Number, obtained through the Joint Tax Board with your BVN and an ID. From January 2026 your National Identity Number serves as your TIN.
  • Income tax. Independent contractors pay Personal Income Tax at progressive rates of roughly 7 to 24 percent. The 2025 reform raised the tax-free threshold to ₦800,000 a year. Clients may withhold 5 to 10 percent at source; the withholding receipt is credited against your annual bill.
  • VAT. Registration is required once annual turnover passes ₦25 million. The rate is 7.5 percent. Some professional-service categories must register regardless of turnover.
  • Filing. Annual assessment and payment, usually by the end of March. Monthly VAT returns if registered.

Official source: Nigeria Revenue Service (formerly FIRS). The tax set-aside calculator has a simple mode for any country; its US mode does not apply to Nigeria.

Frequently asked questions

Should I open a domiciliary account?

If you are paid in dollars regularly, yes. It lets you hold the currency and convert when you choose instead of accepting whatever rate applies on the day the money lands.

A client withheld 10 percent. Is that money gone?

No. Withholding tax paid at source is credited against your annual personal income tax. Keep the withholding receipt.

Do I need to register for VAT?

Only once turnover passes ₦25 million a year, unless you fall into a professional category that must register anyway. Check with the Nigeria Revenue Service.

Which service gives the best naira rate?

It changes week to week. Compare the quoted rate against the mid-market rate and count the fee too; the fee comparison tool makes that arithmetic quick.
