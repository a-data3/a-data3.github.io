Freelancing in Pakistan

Pakistan is one of the largest freelance supplier countries and one of the fastest growing. PayPal does not operate here, so almost everyone is paid through Payoneer or a bank wire, and export income has its own tax regime. This page explains the structure, with a rate calculator in Pakistani rupees.

Getting paid in Pakistan

Payoneer is the primary or backup channel for roughly four in five Pakistani freelancers, withdrawing to a rupee bank account in two to five business days, and its partnership with Meezan Bank allows near-instant in-app withdrawals. Direct SWIFT wires to banks such as HBL, UBL, MCB or Meezan are common for direct clients and carry a fixed fee that hurts on small invoices. PayPal does not support receiving payments in Pakistan. Model your options in the payment fee comparison.

Where Pakistani freelancers find work

Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com and Guru carry most of the work. Local alternatives include Jobbers.io, XpertFlow and WorkChest.

Registration and tax, at a structural level

  • Tax ID. Register for a National Tax Number through the FBR's IRIS portal, choosing the freelancer or professional category. Registration is free and usually done within a few working days.
  • Income tax. IT and IT-enabled export earnings, which is how platform income is classified, are taxed under a final withholding regime rather than the normal slabs: 1 percent of qualifying export proceeds, reduced to 0.25 percent for freelancers registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board, through tax year 2029. At least 80 percent of foreign income must arrive through approved banking channels to keep the reduced rate.
  • Sales tax. Sales tax on services is provincial, and IT export services are generally zero-rated or exempt, so it rarely applies to foreign-client income.
  • Filing. An annual return. Filing puts you on the Active Taxpayers List, which halves the withholding rates you face on banking and asset transactions.

Official source: Federal Board of Revenue and IRIS registration portal. The tax set-aside calculator has a simple mode for any country; its US mode does not apply to Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

Why does everyone use Payoneer?

Because PayPal cannot receive payments in Pakistan and Payoneer is built into Upwork and Fiverr payouts. It withdraws to a local bank in rupees, and the Meezan partnership makes withdrawals nearly instant.

Is freelance income taxed at the normal rates?

Foreign platform income usually counts as IT export income and is taxed at a low final withholding rate instead of the slabs, provided most of it arrives through approved banking channels. Confirm your own case with the FBR.

What does PSEB registration change?

It cuts the final tax on export proceeds from 1 percent to 0.25 percent. It is separate from the NTN registration.

Should I bother filing if tax is withheld anyway?

Yes. Filing keeps you on the Active Taxpayers List, and non-filers pay double withholding on many everyday transactions.
