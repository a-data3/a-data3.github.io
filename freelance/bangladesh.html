Freelancing in Bangladesh

Bangladesh has the largest share of Fiverr's sellers and a fast-growing freelance workforce. Getting foreign currency into a taka account cheaply is the daily problem, and the tax side has changed recently with a government Freelancer ID. This page covers the structure, with a rate calculator in taka.

Getting paid in Bangladesh

Payoneer routed into bKash is now the fastest common method: a bank-intermediary arrangement lets you move a Payoneer balance into bKash instantly with no paperwork. Direct foreign-currency bank accounts are rare, so most freelancers convert to taka and lose a few percent of each payment to bank fees and exchange markups; the spread between banks is real, so it is worth comparing. Use the payment fee comparison with your bank's actual figures.

Where Bangladeshi freelancers find work

Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com for international clients. Belancer, Kajkhuji and Bizzmakr handle smaller taka-denominated local jobs, usually paid through bKash.

Registration and tax, at a structural level

  • Tax ID. A Taxpayer Identification Number from the National Board of Revenue, registered online with your NID. Separately, the voluntary government Freelancer ID (from January 2026) gives access to bank credit and training but does not replace tax registration.
  • Income tax. Once you have a TIN you file every year even if nothing is owed. Foreign-client income received through a Bangladeshi bank can qualify for exemption under NBR conditions, and individual freelancers receive a 2.5 percent export incentive on foreign-currency earnings.
  • VAT. No VAT or turnover tax below roughly Tk 30 lakh of annual turnover, a simplified 3 percent turnover tax in a middle band, and standard 15 percent VAT above the top threshold. Foreign-currency export earnings are zero-rated regardless. Sources differ on the exact top figure, so check current NBR guidance.
  • Filing. Annual return due 30 November for the income year ending 30 June. Monthly VAT returns if registered.

Official source: National Board of Revenue and Freelancer ID portal. The tax set-aside calculator has a simple mode for any country; its US mode does not apply to Bangladesh.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to get Payoneer money into bKash?

Link the two through the official bKash and Payoneer arrangement. Transfers arrive instantly and skip the paperwork of a bank wire.

Is the Freelancer ID the same as tax registration?

No. The Freelancer ID is a voluntary identity card that unlocks credit and training benefits. Tax registration with the NBR is separate and still required.

Do I pay tax on income from foreign clients?

Foreign-currency income received through a bank can qualify for exemption under NBR conditions, and there is an export incentive on top. You still have to file a return each year. Confirm the current conditions with the NBR.

How much do banks take on conversion?

Between roughly 3 and 6 percent once fees and the exchange markup are counted, and it varies by bank. Put the real numbers into the fee comparison tool before you pick one.
